## Option 2: LinkedIn — Professional Announcement

> How many times has your engineering team resolved an outage, only to see the exact same mistake repeated three months later?
>
> When incidents hit, on-call engineers face high cognitive load. Past post-mortems often sit unread in documentation graveyards.
>
> To close this loop, I built **MemoryOps** — an AI Incident Response and Engineering Memory Console.
>
> Here’s what makes MemoryOps different from typical generative AI prototypes:
>
> 1. **Grounded in Immutable Evidence**: Hypotheses and remediation proposals must cite specific logs, metric anomalies, and deployment diffs.
> 2. **Strict Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards**: Remediation is approval-gated and simulator-safe. Execution is never conflated with verification.
> 3. **The Post-Mortem Review Gate**: Automated post-mortems remain in draft status until an operator verifies the facts. Unreviewed records cannot be retained into memory.
> 4. **Institutional Learning with Vectorize Hindsight**: Once approved, lessons are retained in a persistent Hindsight bank. During subsequent incidents, the agent recalls past findings and avoids known dead-ends (e.g., deprioritizing blind service restarts when prior post-mortems proved they fail to hold).
>
> The project is fully open source, built with React 19, Express 5, TypeScript, and the official `@vectorize-io/hindsight-client` SDK, backed by 35 automated tests.
